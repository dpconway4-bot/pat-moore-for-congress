Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

Pat Moore for Congress (“the campaign,” “we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through patmooreforcongress.com, our text messages, our phone calls, and our other campaign activities, and how we use it.

Information we collect

Information you give us. Your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, and ZIP code when you sign up to volunteer, ask for updates, RSVP to an event, or contact us.

Contribution information. When you contribute, federal law requires us to collect your name, mailing address, occupation, and employer. Contributions are processed by ActBlue; your payment card details are collected and handled by ActBlue under its own privacy policy, not stored by the campaign. Federal law prohibits contributions from foreign nationals, and contributions must be made from your own funds.

Website. Like most websites, our site and its hosting provider automatically record basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited, to operate and secure the site.

Publicly available information. We may use public voter registration records and other sources, as permitted by Louisiana law, to contact voters.

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • communicate with you about the campaign, the election, voting, events, and volunteer opportunities;
  • process contributions and meet our legal reporting obligations;
  • organize volunteers and campaign activities; and
  • respond to your questions.

Federal reporting of contributions

Federal law requires us to report to the Federal Election Commission (FEC) the name, mailing address, occupation, and employer of anyone whose contributions total more than $200 in an election cycle. The FEC makes these reports public. Information taken from FEC reports may not be sold or used by anyone for commercial purposes or to solicit contributions.

Text messages (SMS)

If you give us your mobile number and agree to receive text messages, Pat Moore for Congress may send you recurring messages about the campaign, voting, events, and volunteering, and may ask for contributions.

  • Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. You will receive one message confirming you have opted out.
  • Reply HELP for help.
  • Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any contribution or purchase.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with or sold to any third parties, except as required by law.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your information with other campaigns or organizations. We may share it:

  • with service providers that help us run the campaign (for example, our texting, calling, email, website, and contribution-processing vendors), who may use it only to perform services for us; and
  • with the FEC and other government authorities as required by law.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information we collect. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Text messages: reply STOP.
  • Email: use the unsubscribe link in any campaign email.
  • Phone calls: ask the caller to remove you from our list, or contact us.

To ask what information we hold about you, or to have it corrected or removed from our contact lists, contact us using the details below. Information we are legally required to keep, such as contribution records, may be retained.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
