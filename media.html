Summary

Black voters districtwide, as well as voters who supported Democrats in 2026 and 2024 and are very likely to support Democrats now, need to receive mail, see ads on CTV, Youtube and Meta, and hear on the radio about the importance of the December 12th runoff about how:

  • Patricia Moore fights for Louisiana families – working to lower healthcare costs, protect Medicaid, stop the Iran War raising gas and diesel prices, and fight the Trump corruption. Gabe Firment puts special interests first. He supports historic Medicaid cuts that threaten our rural hospitals and backs the Trump Iran War, Trump tariffs, and Trump corruption driving up prices here at home.

Independent voters who are very likely to vote need to receive mail and see ads on CTV, Youtube and Meta about how Gabe Firment will only fight for special interests.

  • Gabe Firment puts big corporations and special interests ahead of Louisiana families. He took campaign cash from data center companies, signed a secret NDA to hide project details from residents, and voted for billion-dollar tax breaks that drain our local water and drive up utility bills. At the same time, Firment pocketed thousands from insurance lobbyists and voted to shield insurers from accountability—making it easier for them to jack up rates and deny claims. While Louisiana families pay the price, Gabe Firment cashes in.
